UPHELD LOGO KIT

Brand colors
- Navy: #052355
- Teal: #07B4A6
- White: #FFFFFF
- Deep Navy: #031536
- Soft Cloud: #F5F7FA

Typeface
- Logo wordmark converted to vector outlines using Inter Display Bold, an approved companion/fallback typeface in the brand guidelines. No font installation is required to use the SVG files.

Folders
- SVG: editable vector masters, plus PDF vector exports of the three core marks
- PNG/Primary: transparent high-resolution color assets
- PNG/Monochrome: navy, teal, black, and white single-color variations
- App_Icons: 16 to 1024 px app/social icon exports
- Favicon: multi-size favicon.ico and PNG favicons
- Preview: visual contact sheet

Usage
- Use color-light variants on white or light neutral backgrounds.
- Use color-dark or white variants on navy/dark backgrounds.
- Maintain clear space around the logo and do not stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, or add effects.
- Teal should remain an accent color rather than the dominant field in most compositions.
